    How to Play

    Goal

    1. Escape your enclosure using your animal's ability.
    2. Sneak behind an unwatched keeper and hold E while moving slowly to steal a key. Stay close throughout. Hold E at a gold cage lock to spend it, or build an escape ramp for your friends.
    3. Restore power: in the Maintenance Yard each player opens their own bay, takes the fuse and plugs it into the generator.
    4. Hold the exit keys in the Exit Plaza at the same time — one key per player.
    5. Everyone walks through the Moonleaf Gate.

    Controls

    • W A S D or arrow keys — move
    • E — interact (hold for levers, cranks and keys)
    • Space — use your ability
    • 1–6 — quick messages to teammates
    • B — bite a keeper, nip an animal, or free a teammate’s muzzle · Esc — menu
    • Touch: left stick to move, E and ★ buttons on the right.

    Keepers

    Keepers walk set routes with flashlights. If one spots you, a ? meter fills — get out of the light! At 100% they shout ! and chase. Hide in bushes, break line of sight behind walls and hedges, or rattle a noisy bin to send them looking somewhere else.

    B bites. A bitten keeper lies unconscious for 30 seconds and can be looted, but all keepers gain tranquilizer darts and permanent high alert for this escape. A dart hit returns you to your enclosure with a muzzle. Muzzles block biting and beaver chewing; a teammate can face you and press B to bite it off. Construction remains possible, even when muzzled.

    No minimap, waypoints, or through-wall keeper highlights: use landmarks and talk to your team. Hard mode, chosen on the title/join screen or in Settings, hides everything behind you and behind obstacles. Forward vision has no distance limit.

    Keepers stay on alert for three minutes after the latest sighting, with wider vision and faster movement. Large animals (elephant, giraffe and zebra) need two adjoining bushes to hide. Keepers radio sightings immediately. Break their line of sight before hiding; entering cover while watched does not hide you.

    Caught animals go back to their enclosure with a few seconds of safety. Escape again, or a teammate can steal a key for your cage or build a ramp. Everything your team finished stays finished.

    Playing together

    Each puzzle splits into one part per player, built for each animal's ability. If a teammate disconnects, the team waits for them; you can choose to continue without them and their remaining task is adapted.
