    Settings

    How to Play

    Goal

    1. Escape your enclosure using your animal's ability.
    2. Sneak behind an unwatched keeper and hold E while moving slowly to steal a key. Stay close throughout. Hold E at a gold cage lock to spend it, or build an escape ramp for your friends.
    3. Restore power: in the Maintenance Yard each player opens their own bay, takes the fuse and plugs it into the generator.
    4. Hold the exit keys in the Exit Plaza at the same time — one key per player.
    5. Everyone walks through the Moonleaf Gate.

    Controls

    • W A S D or arrow keys — move
    • E — interact (hold for levers, cranks and keys)
    • Space — use your ability
    • 1–6 — quick messages to teammates
    • B — bite a keeper, nip an animal, or free a teammate’s muzzle · Esc — menu
    • Touch: left stick to move, E and ★ buttons on the right.

    Keepers

    Keepers walk set routes with flashlights. If one spots you, a ? meter fills — get out of the light! At 100% they shout ! and chase. Hide in bushes, break line of sight behind walls and hedges, or rattle a noisy bin to send them looking somewhere else.

    B bites. A bitten keeper lies unconscious for 30 seconds and can be looted, but all keepers gain tranquilizer darts and permanent high alert for this escape. A dart hit returns you to your enclosure with a muzzle. Muzzles block biting and beaver chewing; a teammate can face you and hold B to bite it off. Construction remains possible, even when muzzled.

    Click the live map in the top right to enlarge or shrink it. Gameplay keeps running while it is open. Talk to your navigator for driving directions. Hard mode, chosen on the title/join screen or in Settings, keeps the map visible but shows guards only within 10 tiles ahead, with a clear line of sight.

    Keepers stay on alert for three minutes after the latest sighting, with wider vision and faster movement. Large animals (elephant, giraffe and zebra) need two adjoining bushes to hide. Keepers radio sightings immediately. Break their line of sight before hiding; entering cover while watched does not hide you.

    Caught animals go back to their enclosure with a few seconds of safety. Escape again, or a teammate can steal a key for your cage or build a ramp. Everything your team finished stays finished.

    City jobs

    Park and climb out to enter Cedar Bank, Second Paw Thrift, or Animal Control. Hold E close to a bank customer to pickpocket some cash. A bite puts a customer to sleep so you can take their remaining money, but calls animal control: leave the bank within two minutes. Everyone must hold E at the vault wheel together to open it. Buy and wear a shirt, hat and pants at the thrift store to blend into the city crowd.

    Captured animals wait in the animal-control cage. Any free teammate can open the outside latch. If everyone is captured, move to the door and each hold E twice for two charged hits. The navigator has a private GPS map and turn instructions; read them aloud to your driver.

    Northgate rescue and airport escape

    Explore the zoo gardens, upstairs terrace and indoor animal houses. Each teammate must find their numbered relay fuse and connect it at the garden generator. The habitat signal house then needs everyone at a separate console, completing five tasks by sharing clues. Some cages need every player to hold a separate numbered handle together. All twelve zoo animals and all four pet-store animals must safely run free before the airport opens.

    Visitors can phone the guards after seeing a release. Their report describes your outfit. Change clothes or break sight and hide in bushes. Captured zoo animals go to the garden holding cage: a free teammate can open the latch; if everyone is trapped, all hold E at separate wall sections together.

    Board the escape plane at Wildwing Airport and operate your own flight controls with the crew. You can climb out before takeoff to help a teammate. Biting is slightly easier and repeated bite presses have no cooldown, but walls, muzzles and the consequences of biting humans still apply.

    Playing together

    Each puzzle splits into one part per player, built for each animal's ability. If a teammate disconnects, the team waits for them; you can choose to continue without them and their remaining task is adapted.
